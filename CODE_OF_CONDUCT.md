# Code of Conduct

AccessAid Lite is intended for public-good accessibility work. Contributors are expected to keep discussion respectful, practical, and focused on helping people improve access to public information.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect different levels of accessibility and technical experience.
- Focus criticism on the work, not the person.
- Be clear about uncertainty and limitations.
- Avoid legal, medical, financial, or compliance advice unless you are explicitly qualified and the project scope supports it.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing secrets, cookies, tokens, credentials, or private user data.
- Misrepresenting AccessAid Lite as a full WCAG audit or legal compliance tool.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may lead to blocked participation.
