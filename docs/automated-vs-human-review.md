# Automated Vs Human Review

AccessAid Lite is a preliminary automated check. It helps teams find common static issues, but it is not a full WCAG audit and does not provide legal or medical advice.

| Review type | Good for | Cannot determine | Who should do it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Automated preliminary check | Common static issues such as missing title, missing alt, skipped headings, generic links, and unlabeled controls | Real usability, legal compliance, screen reader behavior, focus order, contrast in context, dynamic states | Maintainers, volunteers, developers |
| Human accessibility review | Practical barriers, task flow, clarity, assistive technology sanity checks, real user needs | Every WCAG detail unless the reviewer is trained for that scope | Users, reviewers, accessibility-aware staff |
| Full audit | Deeper WCAG assessment, structured evidence, broader risk review | Ongoing maintenance alone | Qualified accessibility professional |

Use AccessAid Lite as a first pass, then involve human reviewers before making accessibility claims.
