# Accessibility Review Request

Hi,

I am looking for a human accessibility reviewer to sanity-check AccessAid Lite, a privacy-first CLI for preliminary static accessibility checks.

Could you review:

- Rule wording.
- Severity choices.
- Remediation clarity.
- Limitations and disclaimers.
- Risk of false confidence.
- Missing disclaimers.
- Whether the docs are clear for small nonprofits, schools, community groups, and open-source maintainers.

Important boundaries:

- This is not a full WCAG audit.
- It does not claim legal compliance.
- It does not provide legal or medical advice.
- Human review is still required.
- The tool does not upload page content, read cookies or tokens, log in, execute JavaScript, or submit forms.

Repo: https://github.com/a78c7/accessaid-lite

Release: https://github.com/a78c7/accessaid-lite/releases/tag/v0.1.1

The most useful feedback would be where the tool might overstate confidence, understate human review needs, or give remediation that is too vague or misleading.
